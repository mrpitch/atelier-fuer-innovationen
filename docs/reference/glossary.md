# Domain Glossary

This site republishes **Xenia**, a 1990s Siemens AG research concept ("Städte des Wissens als Stätten der Begegnung", led by Dr. Helmut Volkmann), under the **Atelier für Innovationen** brand. The terms below are German — they are the project's own vocabulary (used verbatim in page titles, `meta.json` sections, and slugs) and are kept untranslated rather than given an English name; definitions are in English for contributors.

## Language

### The city-of-knowledge concept

**Xenia**:
The research project's own name for its central artifact: a **Wissensstadt** that reframes the transition to the information society as a walkable city, organized as one **Zentrum** plus eight **Stadtviertel**.
_Avoid_: Xeniapolis (this site's docs-section label for Xenia's content, not a name the project itself uses)

**Wissensstadt** (City of Knowledge):
The organizing metaphor of Xenia — a city built from ideas instead of buildings, where each district concentrates on one facet of designing knowledge work for the information society.
_Avoid_: "Wissenstadt" (a misspelling that also appears in some of the source content — don't propagate it in new writing), knowledge city

**Leitbild** ("Städte des Wissens als Stätten der Begegnung" — Cities of Knowledge as Places of Encounter):
Xenia's guiding vision statement, treated as a fixed, quotable phrase throughout the source content rather than a paraphrasable slogan.
_Avoid_: mission statement, vision (too generic — always use the German phrase verbatim when citing it)

**Polis**:
The classical-city ideal Xenia revives as its Leitbild, read simultaneously as a civic community, an economic/institutional network, and a shared cultural memory — not merely "a city".
_Avoid_: city-state

**Zentrum**:
The non-thematic hub at the heart of the Wissensstadt — a deliberately empty, Agora-like coordinating space where visitors orient themselves before dispersing into a Stadtviertel.
_Avoid_: center, downtown

**Stadtviertel** (District):
One of Xenia's eight thematic zones, each built around a single **Gestaltungsschwerpunkt**. Always name which one; "Viertel" alone is ambiguous.
_Avoid_: Viertel (without qualifying which), neighborhood, quarter (as a standalone translation)

**Gestaltungsschwerpunkt** (Design Focus):
The one design concern a Stadtviertel concentrates on within the larger challenge of conceiving knowledge cities — expressed in the source content as a short "Ansatz" (approach) tagline per district.
_Avoid_: theme, topic

### The eight Stadtviertel

| Stadtviertel             | Ansatz (tagline)                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Annäherung               | Städte erleben, Wissen gewinnen!                                                                                             |
| Märkte (und Kooperation) | Innovation aus Tradition, die ersten Weichen sind gestellt.                                                                  |
| Kontexte                 | Städte erleben, Wissen gewinnen!                                                                                             |
| Inszenierung             | Das Vor-Bild der überschaubaren Polis!                                                                                       |
| Zukunft                  | Volkswirtschaftlich richtig rechnen!                                                                                         |
| Wertschöpfung            | Unternehmer, übernehmen Sie!                                                                                                 |
| Potenziale               | Methodische Beherrschung der integrativ zu gestaltenden Prozesse von Kommunikation, Information, Wissen, Innovation, Wandel. |
| Führung                  | Chancen denken lernen!                                                                                                       |

> **Gap:** unlike the other seven, **Märkte** has no dedicated `src/content/docs/xeniapolis/<slug>/` folder yet — it currently exists only as a mention inside `besuch-in-der-wissensstadt.mdx`. Note this if you're about to treat the Stadtviertel list as fully scaffolded.

### Societal frame

**Informationsgesellschaft** (Information Society):
The societal transition Xenia was built to help navigate — the source content is explicit that this is more than "Industriegesellschaft plus Informationstechnik" (industrial society plus information technology bolted on).
_Avoid_: digital society, information age

**Problemlösungsgesellschaft** (Problem-Solving Society):
Xenia's name for the society the Informationsgesellschaft is becoming — one organized around "Problemlösungsgeschäfte" (problem-solving business/ventures) rather than around leisure or consumption.
_Avoid_: knowledge economy (a different, external concept — don't conflate)

### Brand

**Atelier für Innovationen** (Workshop for Innovators):
This site's own name — and, inside the Xenia content itself, the "Stätte der Begegnung" (place of encounter) that innovators use within a Wissensstadt. Treat the brand name and the in-content concept as the same idea, not a coincidence.
_Avoid_: studio, lab
